02. About

Who I Am

Hi, I'm Carlo, a fourth-year BS Information Technology student who combines hands-on technical work with a leader's perspective. I build things, and I think about the full picture behind them: the people, the process, and the problem being solved.

My experience spans web development, databases, mobile apps, networking, and data analytics and I've built and shipped projects across all of these areas. That breadth is intentional. Being fluent across disciplines makes me a faster learner, a stronger communicator, and someone who can contribute meaningfully in any role on a team.

From leading my capstone project to delivering real-world systems, I've taken full ownership from planning and coordination down to writing and shipping the actual product. I believe the most valuable skill in IT is the ability to learn fast, adapt to any stack, and lead with clarity.
